# Master Services Agreement — Northwind Logistics

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between DocuMind Demo Corp ("Client") and Northwind Logistics Ltd ("Supplier") with an effective date of 1 March 2026.

## 1. Scope of Services

Supplier shall provide warehousing, order fulfilment, and last-mile delivery services for the Client's European operations. A detailed description of each service is maintained in Schedule A, which may be amended only by written agreement of both parties.

## 2. Term

The initial term of this Agreement is twenty-four (24) months from the effective date. The Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Termination

3.1 **Termination for convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party.

3.2 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy that breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice describing it.

3.3 **Termination for insolvency.** Either party may terminate immediately if the other party becomes insolvent, enters administration, or has a receiver appointed over a substantial part of its assets.

3.4 **Effect of termination.** On termination, Supplier shall return all Client inventory within fifteen (15) business days and Client shall pay all undisputed fees for services performed up to the termination date. Termination does not relieve either party of obligations accrued before the termination date.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Client shall pay the fees set out in Schedule B. Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears, and Client shall pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 4% per annum above the European Central Bank base rate.

## 5. Service Levels

Supplier shall dispatch 98% of orders received before 14:00 local time on the same business day, measured monthly. If the dispatch rate falls below 95% in any calendar month, Client is entitled to a service credit equal to 5% of that month's fees.

## 6. Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury caused by negligence, or fraud.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret for the term of this Agreement and for five (5) years thereafter, and shall use it solely to perform obligations under this Agreement.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Dublin.
