# Employee Handbook

Last revised 5 February 2026. This handbook summarises the terms that apply to all permanent employees. Where it conflicts with an individual contract of employment, the contract prevails.

## 1. Working Hours

Standard working hours are thirty-seven and a half (37.5) hours per week. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, during which employees are expected to be available for meetings. Hours worked outside core hours are arranged with the line manager.

## 2. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week by default. Fully remote arrangements require director approval and a documented review after six (6) months. Employees working remotely must use company-managed devices and must not access Confidential data over public networks without the company VPN.

## 3. Annual Leave

Employees accrue twenty-five (25) days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to five (5) unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken before 31 March. Leave requests of more than five consecutive days require four (4) weeks' notice.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees receive full pay for the first twenty (20) working days of certified sickness absence in any rolling twelve-month period. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three (3) consecutive working days.

## 5. Parental Leave

Birthing parents are entitled to twenty-six (26) weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents are entitled to eight (8) weeks of paid parental leave, which may be taken in up to two blocks within the first year.

## 6. Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted within sixty (60) days of being incurred, with an itemised receipt for any claim above twenty-five (25) euro. Travel is booked through the company travel platform. Economy class is standard for flights under six (6) hours.

## 7. Probation and Notice

New employees serve a six (6) month probation period, during which either party may terminate with two (2) weeks' notice. After probation, the notice period is one (1) month for employees and increases to three (3) months for director-level roles.

## 8. Performance Reviews

Formal reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Compensation adjustments take effect from 1 July following the April review cycle.
